📋 How to Play

Quick rules reference

  • 1
    Deal: Each player gets 13 cards from 2 shuffled decks (106 cards). One card is revealed as the Secret Joker wildcard.
  • 2
    Turns: On your turn, draw a card (from deck or discard pile), then discard one card.
  • 3
    Sequences: Form runs of 3+ consecutive cards in the same suit. At least 1 must be pure (no jokers).
  • 4
    Triplets: 3+ cards of the same rank. Allowed after you have 2 valid sequences.
  • ★
    Declare: Organize all 13 cards into valid groups (≥2 sequences, ≥1 pure) and declare to win!
  • 🃏
    Jokers: The printed Joker card and all cards of the Secret Joker's rank act as wildcards.
