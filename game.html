📋 Game Rules

🃏 Deck: Two standard 52-card decks + 2 Joker cards = 106 cards total.
🌟 Secret Joker: One random card is revealed — all cards of that rank act as wildcards, along with printed Joker cards.
▶ Your Turn: First draw one card (from deck or top of discard pile), then discard one card.
✅ Pure Sequence: 3+ consecutive cards of the same suit, NO jokers used.
🔵 Impure Sequence: 3+ consecutive same-suit cards where jokers fill gaps.
🟡 Triplet / Set: 3 or more cards of the same rank, different suits.
🏆 Declare: You need ≥2 sequences (at least 1 must be pure). Remaining cards can be sequences or triplets. All 13 cards must be in valid groups.
📊 Grouping: Use Group Mode to drag cards into groups before declaring. Label each group as Sequence or Triplet.
